- **Frontmatter lint in `deploy.yml`** — regression guard. **Trigger:** every push to `main`. **Action:** before the build, `for f in content/*.md; do [ "$(grep -c '^title:' "$f")" -eq 1 ] || { echo "dup frontmatter: $f"; exit 1; }; done`. **Payoff:** the now-fixed Interesting bug can never silently return.

This is the [[2026-08-07]] cadence lever made concrete — and the one structural change a run can propose **without** widening its own write scope. (It's also what Top-3 #1 acts on today.)
