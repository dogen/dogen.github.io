## Patterns I may be missing

- **The 29:1 ratio is the container-vs-contents pattern made numeric.** Every review since ~[[2026-08-21]] has named the same thing — the frame gets built, the inside stays empty. This week it stops being a metaphor: the analysis *about* the garden now outweighs the garden 29 to 1, and the gap widens every Friday whether or not a single word is added inside. The reviews are a genuinely honest mirror, but a mirror that only a cron job and (maybe) you ever read, stacking up in a `draft: true` folder that never ships. That's worth sitting with: the one part of this whole loop that reliably *produces writing every week* is the part that writes **about** the vault rather than **in** it.
- **The human-reminder experiment is now conclusively closed.** [[2026-10-02]] gave the cheapest possible asks (paste three blocks into open, healthy files). [[2026-10-09]] found them untaken after a week and isolated the bottleneck: not willpower, taste, or time — nothing in the loop writes to the content files except you, and that write just doesn't happen even at the two-minute scale. Two weeks on, the result is unchanged. There is no remaining version of "a better-worded reminder" worth trying; the evidence is in.
- **So the only recommendation that still fits is the one every recent review has circled: let the scheduled run make the mechanical write.** Scoped tightly — duplicated frontmatter, a lowercase heading it flagged, a commented-empty section it has a seed for, a build in the repo that isn't logged in [[projects]]. Not prose, ever. I did **not** grant myself that access (a scheduled run with nobody watching isn't authorization to widen its own permissions), but if one thing changes about how these reviews operate, this is it — and the 29:1 number is the clearest case yet that *noticing* has hit its ceiling.
- **Cadence: fourteenth consecutive weekly; this is exactly the empty cycle the [[2026-08-07]] rule was written for.** Event-triggered ("run on a non-review content commit") or monthly would have skipped this week with nothing lost — and would also stop the meta-pile from doubling the vault on quiet weeks. Standing recommendation, not re-litigated.
